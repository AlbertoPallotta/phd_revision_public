checks and the 9007 baseline in its historical workspace. Those prerequisites
have not been removed or satisfied by copying old PASS files. It is not a
download-only command. The adapter validates selected saved plotting CSVs and
does not run a strategy or use a price-containing result object.
Historical A1/B3 execution: original 21 October 0.1.4.9008 archive, separately
identified in manifests/execution-identities.csv. No new study is implied.

A1 uses unchanged daily/weekly samples, 39 grouped four-week dates, momentum12,
daily population-SD60, Top5 OR Top5, and 60/40 positive-momentum/reciprocal-sigma
weights over the union. Volatility must be finite and positive. An empty leg
leaves 100% target budget for the other; two empty legs leave cash.

B3 full sample: L=8,12,16,20; N=5,10,15 (12 evaluations).
B3 WF: L=8,12,26; N=5,10; IS52/OOS26/step26; four windows (24 IS + 4 OOS).
Propagate momentum in both. IS startup cash is scored. OOS receives a preceding
26-row IS tail for indicators. Each OOS account resets to100000; stitching
compounds only within-leg intervals, not continuous holdings across boundaries.
Same-row supplied prices, whole shares, residual zero-interest cash, gross/no
costs/no stops. A1 + B3 = 41 evaluations. No tuning or historical equivalence
claim. Saved older results retain their original execution versions.
