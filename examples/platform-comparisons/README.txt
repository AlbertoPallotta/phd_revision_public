COMPARISON SOURCES — EXTERNAL TO THE R PACKAGES

Files prefixed original_ or containing _P<number> are the exact original Word
listing extracts. They preserve the submission record; they are not newly
certified working programs. Other files are selected current illustrations.
Short Word excerpts sometimes condense names/initialisation. The full source
and configuration determine execution. Do not run an excerpt as a full program.

Chapter 2: amended/current illustrative listings and preserved originals. Python
and R syntax checked; no new download-based runs. Historical loaders may require
their original environments. EasyLanguage requires its native platform.
Chapter 3 simple: source simple_crossover.R in R; call run_simple_crossover() on
ordered POSIXct OHLC bars with the accepted TradeTesterR installed. Python class
requires Backtrader and an explicit data feed/broker. Selected synthetic harnesses
are in historical-checks/; dated evidence is indexed in
../../environment/HISTORICAL_TECHNICAL_INDEX.txt.
Chapter 3 advanced: source advanced_tradetester.R in a data.table/TTR environment;
the preparation function is the original rule. Completed advanced_backtrader.py
is the previously tested native illustration. Complete historical fixtures
and workspaces are not supplied; the historical-access and runner-contracts
manifests identify execution prerequisites and limitations.
Chapter 4: load PortfolioTesteR/data.table and quantstrat, source
chapter4/portfolio_comparisons.R. comparison_targets() returns both recipes and
intermediate panels. Supply prices and weights_a or weights_b to run_backtest()
or account_targets(..., id=<a fresh name>). The accounting comparator installs
symbol price objects in its R session; use a fresh process as the check runner
does. It does not simulate an order queue.

Historical launchers can retain their original local paths and environment
requirements. Copying these sources does not provide those workspaces or
inputs. Use ../../manifests/runner-contracts.csv and
../../manifests/historical-access.csv for the supported execution/access routes.
