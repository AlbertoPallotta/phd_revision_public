{ One share; multiplier one. Native platform configuration is required.
  Documentation-checked illustration; not compiled or executed here. }
Inputs: FastLength(20), SlowLength(50), StopLossPct(2);
Variables: FastMA(0), SlowMA(0);
FastMA = Average(Close, FastLength);
SlowMA = Average(Close, SlowLength);
If MarketPosition = 0 and FastMA crosses above SlowMA then
    Buy 1 share next bar at market;
If MarketPosition > 0 then begin
    SetStopPosition;
    SetStopLoss(StopLossPct * EntryPrice / 100);
    If FastMA crosses below SlowMA then Sell next bar at market;
End;
